5 Feeding Mistakes That Kill Catfish Growth

1. Wrong Feed Size: Fingerlings need 2mm feed. Growers need 4mm. Using big feed for small fish = waste.

2. Overfeeding: If feed stays after 30 mins, you’re polluting your pond and wasting money.

3. Irregular Time: Feed 2 times daily. 8am and 5pm. Catfish grow 40% faster with routine.

4. Poor Protein: Use 45% for fingerlings, 42% for growers, 38% for table size.

5. Feeding During Stress: Don't feed after rain or when water is bad.
