How to Feed Catfish for Maximum Growth

Posted: July 2026 | By: Lagos Fresh Catfish Farm

Growing big, healthy catfish in Lagos is 80% about feeding. If you get feeding wrong, you will waste money and your fish will grow slowly.

1. The 3 Stages of Feeding

Fingerlings 0-4 weeks: Feed 4-5 times daily with 2mm starter feed. 40% protein.

Juveniles 1-3 months: Feed 3 times daily with 3mm grower feed. 35% protein.

Table Size 3-4 months: Feed 2 times daily with 4.5mm finisher feed. 30% protein.

2. The 5% Rule

Only feed 3-5% of your total fish body weight per day. Example: If you have 100kg of fish, feed 3kg to 5kg of feed daily. Split into 2 meals: 8AM and 5PM.

3. 3 Mistakes Lagos Farmers Make

  • Overfeeding: Causes dirty water and disease. If you see feed floating after 10 minutes, you fed too much.
  • Feeding at wrong time: Feed early morning and late evening when water is cool.
  • Cheap feed: Use Coppens, Aqua or Vital feed. Cheap feed = slow growth.
