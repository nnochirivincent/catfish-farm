How to Maintain Perfect Water Quality for Catfish

pH: Keep between 6.5 - 8.0. Test every 2 days.

Ammonia: The #1 killer. Do 30% water change twice weekly.

Oxygen: Use aerator at night. Catfish gasp at surface = low oxygen.

Temperature: Best is 26-30°C for Clarias Gariepinus growth.
