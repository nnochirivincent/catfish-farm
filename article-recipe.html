3 Best Ways to Cook Fresh Catfish

1. Catfish Pepper Soup: Boil with uziza, scent leaf, and pepper. 20 mins and done.

2. Grilled Catfish: Marinate with garlic, ginger, maggi. Grill for 25 mins.

3. Catfish Stew: Fry first, then add to tomato stew. Perfect with rice.
