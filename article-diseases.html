5 Common Catfish Diseases & How to Prevent Them

Posted: July 2026 | By: Lagos Fresh Catfish Farm

Disease is the #1 reason farmers lose money. Here are the 5 diseases we see most in Lagos ponds:

1. Fin Rot

Signs: Torn fins, red edges. Cause: Dirty water. Solution: Change 50% of water + add salt.

2. Gills Disease

Signs: Fish gasping at surface. Cause: Low oxygen. Solution: Increase aeration and reduce feeding.

3. Bacterial Infection

Signs: Swollen belly, ulcers. Cause: Overcrowding. Solution: Isolate sick fish + antibiotic feed.

4. Parasites

Signs: Fish rubbing on pond wall. Cause: New fish introduced. Solution: Salt bath for 10 minutes.

5. Fungus

Signs: White cotton-like growth. Cause: Injury. Solution: Malachite green treatment.

Prevention Tip: Change water weekly, don't overfeed, and buy healthy fingerlings from us.
