Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our catfish & fish farming

🐟 How long does it take for catfish to grow to table size?

With proper feeding, our Clarias Gariepinus reaches 1kg table size in 4-5 months. We feed high-protein feed twice daily and change pond water regularly to ensure fast, healthy growth.

🚚 Do you deliver across Lagos? How fast?

Yes! We offer same-day delivery within Lagos. Orders placed before 12pm are delivered same day. Delivery fee depends on your location. For outside Lagos, we can arrange waybill.

💊 Are your catfish treated with antibiotics?

No. Our catfish are 100% antibiotic-free. We use natural pond management, probiotics, and clean water to prevent disease. This makes our fish healthier and safer for your family.

📦 How do you package the fish for delivery?

Live catfish: Delivered in oxygenated water bags. Fresh/Tablesize: Cleaned, gutted and ice-packed. Smoked: Well-dried and sealed in nylon to stay fresh for weeks.

🌱 Do you sell fingerlings and give training?

Yes! We sell healthy fingerlings with 95% survival rate. Every fingerling purchase comes with a FREE feeding guide and 1-month WhatsApp support from our farm manager.

💰 What is your return/refund policy?

If any fish arrives dead or not as described, send us a photo within 30 minutes of delivery and we will replace it or refund you immediately.
